swallow myself

i started this piece shortly after finishing force fed. initially i wanted it to be a continuation, or part of a series but i think its pretty strong on its own.

i didn't have much of a plan going into this piece like i did with force fed, i just knew i wanted hollow tired eyes. the rest just happened as i drew.

i did this piece digitally on my ipad using procreate, although i would like to eventually remake it with charcoal and ink.
