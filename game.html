hi welcome to my untitled game where you kill monstrous misogynist public figures. the goal is to simply hit the blobs, kill them, and reveal their identity. Please note that this game only functions on computer, thanks!

instructions !!
wasd keys to move around
left mouse button to throw an axe
right mouse button to throw a book (knowledge yay)
space bar for sword (close combat)
when youve killed a monster, approach the corpse and hit space bar to reveal identity & learn a fact/quote about them
the hp bar at the top represents your health, don't let them get too close to you
the level bar shows how much xp you've earned from killing. more xp means dealing more damage
currently, there are only 9 possible public figures you can encounter, there will be many more coming soon!
the identity revealed is random so that means you could kill the same person multiple times in the same run
thats pretty much it, enjoy!!

the following are my notes/research connections for this project
panopticism - michel foucault
i am interested in foucault's discussion of surveillance, visibility, and power. i think the "modern" panopticon is basically electronic surveillance and computer monitoring. there is so much data-driven surveillance by governments and corporations, ultimately leading to people altering their behavior. i like the idea of reversing the relationship between the observer and the observed. public figures normally occupy highly visible positions where they can broadcast their opinions to an audience and make changes / cause harm to large groups of people. in my game, they become the objects of the player's attention and are observed, selected, and targeted.

the gaze/feminist theory
i am also interested in theories surrounding the gaze and who gets to look versus who becomes the object of looking.
since the game focuses on misogynist public figures, i wanted to turn them into objects of the player's gaze.

situationist international
i like the concept of detournement art that takes pre-existing images and media from the ruling class to mock and highlight the underlying ideology of the original idea. in this game, i am taking the public figures as existing cultural imagery by using their public personas and recontextualizing them by putting them in a pixel game.
